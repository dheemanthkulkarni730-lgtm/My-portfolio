    Section 1: Foundations of Hindustani Classical Music Preface
  • Hindustani Classical Music is one of the world's oldest living musical traditions. It has evolved over thousands of years through Vedic chanting, the Nāṭyaśāstra, medieval treatises, and the contributions of numerous musicians and gharanas.At the heart of this tradition lies the Raga, a melodic framework that evokes specific emotions, times of day, and aesthetic experiences.
  • This encyclopedia is designed as a reference for students, performers, teachers, and music enthusiasts.
  • What is a Raga?
  • A Raga is not merely a scale. It is a melodic framework with specific rules governing:
    • Ascending movement (Aroha) Descending movement (Avaroha) Important notes Characteristic phrases Ornamentation Emotional expression (Rasa) Time of performance Every raga has its own identity and personality. Examples: Miyan ki Todi Multani Gujari Todi Important Terms Aroha: Ascending movement of the raga. Avaroha: Descending movement. Pakad: Signature phrase that identifies the raga. Chalan: Typical melodic movement. Vadi: Most important note. Samvadi: Second most important note. Nyas: Note where melodic phrases naturally rest. Jati: Number of notes used in ascent and descent. Alap: Slow, rhythm-free introduction. Bandish: Fixed composition in a raga. Tan: Fast melodic passages.
